# Surface: DPS Calculator

Mode: Operate. Audience: endgame theorycrafters on a PC second monitor and on a phone (full editing on both). Jobs: tweak a build and watch DPS move, audit the math, read the stat sheet. The gear grid and the full stat sheet must be on screen together; no data hidden behind extra clicks. World: Night Transit Signage from DESIGN.md (unchanged); DPS line is orange #f2762e.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The calculator is the game's character screen: equipment on the left, the character sheet on the right. It refuses the old four-column dashboard with tabs hiding the simulator.

OWN-WORLD: The established sign world. Black ground, sign-white Archivo, 3px white rule under the DPS readout, 1px seams between stat rows, outline chips that fill white when active, orange as the DPS line accent (sliders, Math focus). The gear slots keep the game's rarity card art.

STORY: A player loads or names a loadout, swaps a slot in the drawer, and reads the stat rows and DPS change beside it. Math opens the formula audit; Target & assumptions opens inline; pinning a baseline turns on DPS deltas.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Loadout name (editable, 22px) with preset select, Save, JSON and Reset on one row. Left half: Gear / Deviation / Cradle & Food tabs over the weapon and armor grid. Right half: Sustained and Burst DPS at 34px over a 3px white rule with Math on the right, the Weapon 1/2 toggle, then Basic and Battle stat columns. On phones the DPS readout is a sticky black bar under the nav and the sheet follows the grid.

FORM: Character Screen, #1 of my 7 grounded structures, dealt in the surface roll. seed:19fd2e73b71cbd2c.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: stat rows expand in place to list each stat's sources, so the sheet is also the audit. Motion: none beyond the existing drawer.
