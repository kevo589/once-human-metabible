# Surface: Meta Builds

Mode: Operate. Audience: endgame theorycrafters. Jobs: find the best build for their stage now, then follow its full setup while gearing in-game. At a glance each build must show its weapon and Deviation art plus stage and archetype. World: Night Transit Signage (DESIGN.md); Builds line is red #e2332e.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Builds sorted into three stage columns of art cards, with the picked build's whole setup as a station sign beside them. This refuses the old two-wide grid of dense text cards.

OWN-WORLD: The established sign world. Black ground, Archivo, 3px sign-white rules under the stage headings, panel cards with a 2px seam border that turns sign-white when selected, the white-header station sign for the setup, and yellow-outlined breakpoint notes. Weapon renders and Deviation portraits are the content art.

STORY: A player filters by archetype or scenario, scans the Early, Mid and End columns by art, picks a card and reads the full recipe on the sign (weapons, Deviation, armor, mods, cradle, Chef food and drink, breakpoints, anti-synergies, playstyle). Open in DPS calculator loads exactly that build, at the DPS the card shows.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Title with count, plus archetype and scenario selects. Desktop (xl): three stage columns and a 400px sticky sign on the right showing the first End game build. Below xl the columns stack (three across from md) and the sign is a bottom sheet that opens when a card is tapped.

FORM: Stage Columns, #6 of my 7 grounded structures, dealt in the surface roll. seed:df6a55b9145d9271.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: card to sign to calculator. Picking a card fills the sign, and Open in DPS calculator lands on the same build and number. Motion: the sheet's single-axis slide (station-sign) below xl only.
